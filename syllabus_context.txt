SINGAPORE GCE A-LEVEL ECONOMICS SYLLABUS REFERENCE

H1 ECONOMICS (8843) & H2 ECONOMICS (9757)

THEME 1: THE CENTRAL ECONOMIC PROBLEM
1.1 Scarcity, Choice and Opportunity Cost
1.2 Rational Decision Making (Marginalist Principle)

THEME 2: MARKETS (MICROECONOMICS)
2.1 Price Mechanism and its Applications
    - Demand and Supply analysis
    - Elasticities (PED, YED, PES, XED [H2 Only])
    - Consumer and Producer Surplus
2.2 Market Failure
    - Public Goods (Non-excludability, Non-rivalry)
    - Externalities (Positive/Negative in Consumption/Production)
    - Merit and Demerit Goods
    - Information Failure (Asymmetric Information [H2 Only])
    - Immobility of Factors of Production [H2 Only]
    - Policies to correct market failure (Taxes, Subsidies, Regulation, Direct Provision, etc.)
2.3 Firms and Decisions [H2 Only]
    - Costs and Revenue (Short run vs Long run)
    - Profiit Maximisation
    - Market Structures:
        - Perfect Competition
        - Monopolistic Competition
        - Oligopoly (Kinked Demand Curve, Collusion, Price Wars)
        - Monopoly (Natural Monopoly, Price Discrimination)
        - Alternative Objectives of Firms (Growth, Market Share, Satisficing)

THEME 3: THE NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL ECONOMY (MACROECONOMICS)
3.1 Introduction to Macroeconomics
    - Circular Flow of Income
    - Aggregate Demand (AD) and Aggregate Supply (AS) analysis
3.2 Macroeconomic Aims and Issues
    - Standard of Living (Material & Non-material)
    - Economic Growth (Actual vs Potential, Sustainable, Inclusive)
    - Unemployment (Types, Causes, Consequences)
    - Price Stability (Inflation/Deflation: Causes, Consequences)
    - Balance of Payments (Current/Capital/Financial Account) [H2 detailed, H1 awareness]
3.3 Macroeconomic Policies
    - Fiscal Policy (Discretionary vs Automatic Stabilisers)
    - Monetary Policy (Interest Rates vs Exchange Rates [Singapore context: MAS focus on Exchange Rate])
    - Supply-side Policies

THEME 3 (CONTINUED) - INTERNATIONAL ECONOMICS [H2 Emphasis]
3.4 Globalisation and the International Economy
    - Globalisation (Causes, Consequences)
    - Theory of Comparative Advantage
    - Patterns of Trade
    - Protectionism (Tariffs, Quotas, Subsidies)
    - Free Trade Agreements (FTAs)

EXAMINATION SKILLS (Use these for Scaffolding):
- **Knowledge & Understanding (AO1)**: Definitions, Concepts.
- **Interpretation & Evaluation (AO2)**: Application to context/case.
- **Analysis (AO3)**: Economic reasoning, logical chains of analysis.
- **Evaluation (AO4)**: Critical assessment, judgment, synthesis (e.g., Short run vs Long run, prioritizing policies).
